Privacy Policy

FlipTake by UstozAI. Last updated October 18, 2026.

In short: FlipTake does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your videos stay on your iPhone and in your own photo library.

What FlipTake does

FlipTake is a video camera that switches between the front and back cameras while recording, into one video. Everything it does happens on your iPhone.

Camera and microphone

FlipTake asks for camera and microphone access to record video with sound. While the camera screen is open, the microphone is active so that a video has sound from the very first moment you press record. Sound and picture are only saved when you record, and only into your video. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Your photo library

FlipTake asks for photo library access to save your videos to Photos, and to open the videos you recorded so you can watch or delete them in the app. It does not read or upload anything else in your library.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your most recent videos. Copies of your last few recordings are kept in FlipTake's private storage on your iPhone, so you can watch them in the app. Older ones are removed automatically, and deleting FlipTake removes them all.
  • Your settings. Choices such as resolution, frame rate and grid are saved on your iPhone so they are there next time.
  • Details inside each video. Like videos from Apple's Camera, each video records the date and time, the iPhone model and the lens used, so apps like Photos can show them. FlipTake never adds your location.

What FlipTake does not do

  • It does not collect or send any data to UstozAI or anyone else.
  • It does not use your location.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not require an account or sign in.

Your choices

You can turn camera, microphone and photo library access on or off at any time in the Settings app, under FlipTake. You can delete your videos in Photos, and deleting FlipTake removes everything it kept on your iPhone.

Children

FlipTake collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If FlipTake ever starts collecting data, this policy will say exactly what and why before that version is released.
