Recording

How do I switch cameras while recording?

While recording, tap the flip button at the bottom right, or double tap anywhere on the picture. FlipTake keeps recording into the same video. You can switch as often as you like.

What happens at the moment of the switch?

The picture holds for about a third of a second while the other camera starts, and the sound never stops. You end up with one continuous video, with no editing needed.

Can I pause a recording?

Yes. Tap the pause button at the bottom left while recording, and tap it again to continue. Everything goes into the same video.

How do I change the resolution or frame rate?

Tap the format label at the top of the camera, or open FlipTake's settings (the ··· button, then Settings) and choose Record Video. FlipTake records in 4K or HD at 24, 30 or 60 frames per second, depending on what your iPhone supports. Under Formats, High Efficiency makes smaller files and Most Compatible plays on more devices.

Why does my music stop when FlipTake is open?

FlipTake keeps the microphone ready while the camera is open, so your videos have sound from the first moment you press record. iOS pauses other audio while an app uses the microphone. Your music can resume when you leave FlipTake.

Your videos

Where are my videos saved?

Every video is saved to your Photos library automatically. To watch your last video in FlipTake, tap the thumbnail at the bottom left of the camera. Swipe up on it for details like the date and lens, and swipe down to go back to the camera.

Does FlipTake upload my videos?

No. FlipTake never connects to the internet. Your videos stay on your iPhone and in your photo library. See the Privacy Policy.

Permissions

I said no to camera, microphone or photos access. How do I fix it?

Open the Settings app, scroll down to FlipTake, and turn on Camera, Microphone and Photos. Then open FlipTake again.

Requirements

FlipTake runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
